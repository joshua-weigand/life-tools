True cost = depreciation (price minus vehicle value at the end of your horizon) + loan interest paid + insurance + fuel/energy + maintenance & repairs + registration + one-time taxes and fees. This deliberately excludes loan principal, since principal payments convert cash into vehicle equity you already get credit for through resale value — counting both would double-count that cost. Actual depreciation, repair costs, and fuel prices vary by make, model, and location.
